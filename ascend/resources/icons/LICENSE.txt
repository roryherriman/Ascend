Ascend icon set
===============

All SVG files in this directory (ascend/resources/icons/) are original,
hand-authored geometric line icons created for the Ascend project. They do
not reproduce or derive from Blizzard Entertainment, Warcraft Logs, Discord,
Raider.IO, or any other third-party icon set, logo, or game artwork.
